Test mode

Press Test in attract mode. Each press moves on a stage: switches, then coils (flippers choose, Start fires), then lamps (flippers choose, Start lights them all), then back to attract mode. These are the lines the controller prints on its USB serial port, newest first.

What opens the flaps on the original isn't known yet (#24), so open them by hand. Click an open trapdoor to drop a ball through and collect the bonus.

How to play

  • Press Enter to start a game. Press it again during ball 1 to add players, up to 4.
  • Hold Space to pull the plunger back, and let go to launch the ball. The longer you hold, the harder it goes.
  • Hold Z or M for the flippers. N nudges the table.
  • The ball runs on the real rules: it closes the switches it touches, and the game fires the coils that kick it.
  • You can still click lanes, bumpers and rubbers to score by hand. Click the outhole, or press D, to end a ball.
  • Orange glow = coil firing. Dim lamps are off. An open trapdoor glows.
  • Sound starts after your first click or key press (browsers require it).
