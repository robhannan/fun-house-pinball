Trapdoors

What opens the flaps on the original isn't known yet (#24), so open them by hand. Click an open trapdoor to drop a ball through.

How to play

  • Press Enter to start a game.
  • Hold Z or M for the flippers.
  • Click lanes, bumpers and the gate to score.
  • Click the outhole (or press D) to drain the ball.
  • Orange glow = coil firing. Dim lamps are off. An open trapdoor glows.
