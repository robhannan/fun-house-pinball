Playfield tracer

Lines a photo of the real playfield up with the map, so you can drag the map's assumed shapes onto it. The photo stays in your browser: nothing is uploaded.

1. Photo

  • Glass off, the whole board in the frame.
  • Straight down, from as high as you can.
  • Use the phone's 2× lens: the wide one bends the edges.
  • Even light, no flash glare.

2. Line it up

Click each part's centre on the photo. Skip any you can't see. Click a row to redo it. At least four are needed; more is better.

    3. Trace

    Click a shape to select it, then drag its handles onto the photo. Double-click an edge to add a corner, or a corner to remove it. A curve's yellow handle bends it. Blue parts were measured and are locked.
